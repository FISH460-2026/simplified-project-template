How does temperature stress impact the ability of hemigrapsus to detect predator cues of carcinus?

 # Materials list
 - 12 Hemigrapsus oregonensis
 - 2 Carcinus maenus
 - 2 tanks
   - tank1: ambient salinity (35ppt), ambient temp (14C), 6 H. oregonensis, 1 EGC
   - tank2: ambient salinity (35ppt), elevated temp (24C), 6 H. oregonensis, 1 EGC

## Response variables
- Metabolic activity (resazurin assay materials)
- Hemolymph glucose activity (cayman chemical glucose assay)
- Righting time
